Simple & Transparent

How KisanMitra Works

A simple, direct process connecting Indian farms directly to household and business buyers.

For Farmers

Sell your harvest directly

1

Register & Verify

Sign up and complete basic verification to gain trust badge recognition with city buyers.

2

List Produce

Add crop name, quality grade, quantity, and set your own price backed by mandi rate references.

3

Receive Orders

Get instant order notifications on your phone. Accept or schedule fulfillment easily.

4

Dispatch & Earn

Hand over produce for delivery and receive secure, guaranteed payment directly.

For Buyers

Fresh produce delivered to your doorstep

1

Browse the Mandi

Explore farm-fresh produce with clear price breakdowns and origin details.

2

Order Directly

Select quantities with no minimum order hurdles and choose home delivery or pickup.

3

Pay Securely

Checkout with Razorpay online payment or choose Cash on Delivery with zero risk.

4

Track Delivery

Get real-time updates as your order transitions from farm dispatch to your doorstep.
