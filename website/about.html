The Challenge

Broken Supply Chains Hurt Farmers

For decades, smallholder farmers across India have been trapped by an opaque 4-to-6 tier intermediary chain. Produce passes from village aggregators to commission agents, APMC traders, and wholesalers before reaching buyers.

By the time produce arrives in cities, prices inflate by up to 300%, yet the farmer who invested months of hard labor receives only 20-30% of the consumer rupee.

The Solution

Direct, Transparent Digital Mandi

KisanMitra creates a direct bridge. Farmers set their own farm-gate prices backed by live APMC Agmarknet benchmark data. Buyers purchase directly, knowing exactly where their food came from.
